About and quality

Who we are

Mamma’s Food is a leading Egyptian company in the manufacturing, international trade, export, import and distribution of premium frozen fruit, vegetables and food commodities, with production facilities in the 10th of Ramadan industrial area.

Headquartered in New Cairo, with sales offices in Sweden, the United States, Kuwait, Jordan and Italy, we are a reliable partner to food manufacturers and companies across the global frozen food industry.

Our vision

To preserve our heritage and become a leading Egyptian manufacturer of high-quality goods in the food and beverage industry, and the supplier of choice for our type of products.

Our mission

To produce and develop high-quality products at a reasonable cost, and to give our customers the highest level of integrity and satisfaction.

Quality and certification

Food safety systems

HACCP, GMP, SPC and QA systems are applied in manufacturing, storage and other operations. The system is certified for BRC.

Halal

Our products are approved for Halal.

No chemical preservatives

Our frozen vegetables are free from chemical preservatives, with uniform color, texture, flavor and taste.

Pesticide residues

In conformance with WHO recommendations and EC directives.

Export operations

We handle storage, container stuffing and sea freight, and export to Europe, the United Arab Emirates and the GCC in line with Incoterms.

Why buyers choose us

High-demand products, a strong supplier network, competitive pricing, fast export operations and client-focused flexibility.
