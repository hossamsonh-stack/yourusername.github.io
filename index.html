About us

Mamma’s Food is a leading Egyptian company in the manufacturing, international trade, export, import and distribution of premium frozen fruit, vegetables and food commodities. We deliver quality and freshness from our production facilities in the 10th of Ramadan industrial area.

Headquartered in New Cairo, with sales offices in Sweden, the United States, Kuwait, Jordan and Italy, we are a reliable partner to food manufacturers and companies across the global frozen food industry.

Our vision

To preserve our heritage and become a leading Egyptian manufacturer of high-quality goods in the food and beverage industry, and the supplier of choice for our type of products.

Our mission

To produce and develop high-quality products at a reasonable cost, and to give our customers the highest level of integrity and satisfaction.
